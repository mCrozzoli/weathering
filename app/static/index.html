How it connects

Start

  1. Install the boards once. Open Boards, plug each ESP32 in by USB and install its part: Sensor box, Hub, Sculpture. Needs Chrome or Edge.
  2. Power the sensor box and the sculpture. They find the hub by themselves (no addresses to type). The servos need their own 5–6 V supply.
  3. Plug the hub into the computer and double-click Weathering.command. This page opens and connects. Three dots in the top bar turn green: hub, sensor, sculpture.
  4. Leave it. A reading arrives every 5 minutes and is saved at once. At 06:00 and 18:00 the data is read again from the start and the sculpture takes its new shape.

If something stops

  1. Page closed: readings keep being saved by the app. Open http://127.0.0.1:8770 again, or double-click Weathering.command.
  2. Computer off or crashed: double-click Weathering.command. The show reopens where it was; a run that was missed happens at once. The sculpture kept its shape meanwhile, and the sensor box kept about a day of readings and sends them now.
  3. For weeks unattended (Mac mini): System Settings → Energy → Start up automatically after a power failure; add Weathering.command to Login Items; turn off sleep.

No hardware yet? Click Connect → Demo: a pretend room and sculpture, as fast as you like. Everything above works the same.

Boards

Plug one ESP32 in by USB, then click Connect a board. Install its part, change its settings, test it. One board at a time.

Setting up boards needs Chrome or Edge on a computer.

The app is holding the hub's USB port. To set up the hub itself, click Connect → Disconnect first.
